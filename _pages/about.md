I'm a Ph.D. student in Machine Learning at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/), advised by [Dr. Nils Lukas](https://nilslukas.github.io/). I work on **secure and trustworthy machine learning**, with a focus on watermarking and collaborative learning. Previously, I completed my M.Sc. at MBZUAI and was a research intern at CISPA's [SprintML Lab](https://sprintml.com/).

**Highlights**
* [ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/bakr26a.html): threshold watermarking for federated learning.
* [ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/5a1667459d0cdeb2fe6b2f0dffc5cb9d-Abstract-Conference.html): data provenance for image autoregressive models.
* [NeurIPS 2024 Erasing the Invisible](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21072): first place in both tracks.
