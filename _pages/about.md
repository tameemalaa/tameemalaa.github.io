## 👋 Welcome

I’m **Tameem Bakr**, a Ph.D. student in Machine Learning at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/), supervised by [Dr. Nils Lukas](https://nilslukas.github.io/). I work on **secure and trustworthy machine learning**, with a focus on watermarking and collaborative learning. Before starting my Ph.D., I completed my M.Sc. in Machine Learning at MBZUAI.

My recent work includes threshold watermarking for federated learning ([ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/bakr26a.html)), data provenance for image autoregressive models ([ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/5a1667459d0cdeb2fe6b2f0dffc5cb9d-Abstract-Conference.html)), and the first-place solution in both tracks of the [NeurIPS 2024 Erasing the Invisible](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21072) watermark removal challenge. I also study attacks and defenses for machine learning systems, such as prompt injection, jailbreaks, and backdoors.

In 2025, I was a **Research Intern at the [CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security](https://cispa.de/)** in the [SprintML Lab](https://sprintml.com/), where I worked with [Dr. Adam Dziedzic](https://adam-dziedzic.com/) and [Dr. Franziska Boenisch](https://franziska-boenisch.de/) on watermarking and data provenance for image autoregressive models.

If you're interested in collaborating with me, feel free to [connect on LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/tameemalaa), browse my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/tameemalaa), or send me an [email](mailto:tameem.alaa@gmail.com). My CV is available [here](/files/tameem_bakr_cv.pdf).
